# Support

## Where to ask

| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| A usage question or an open-ended idea | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/x1zzdev/Xdaptive/discussions) |
| A confirmed bug or a concrete feature request | [Issues](https://github.com/x1zzdev/Xdaptive/issues) |
| A design decision that needs a record | [docs/rfc/](docs/rfc/) — open an issue labelled `rfc` first |
| A security problem | [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), privately |
| A contribution workflow question | [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) |

Discussions is the right place for "how would this work" and "what if"
questions. The issue tracker is for things that can be closed with a commit.

## Before you ask

- Read [README.md](README.md), the [glossary](docs/00-glossary.md) and the
  [open questions](docs/16-open-questions.md). Many design questions are already
  written down as open decisions rather than answered.
- Search open and closed issues. The problem may already be known.
- For a bug, prepare a minimal reproduction and the exact command you ran.

## What to expect

Xdaptive is maintained by a small number of people and is at Stage 0. Questions
usually get a reply within a few days. Feature requests are read, but not every
one is accepted: the [principles](docs/15-principles.md) are the filter, and a
change that weakens the AI/OS split or makes the system harder to measure is
declined even when it is popular.

## Commercial support

There is none. Xdaptive is a community project with no support contracts, no
SLAs, and no paid tier.
